Three queues, one fair pipeline

General

The default line. Everyone who checks in starts here — first in, first out.

Winners

Players coming off a win queue here, so winners get to play winners.

Losers

Players coming off a loss re-enter here. Nobody disappears for the night after a bad game.

You control re-entry: winners can jump to the front (fairness-first), go to the back (end-of-queue), or take a smart middle position.

Rotating partners built in

Doubles open play is about variety. DinkMatch tracks who has partnered with whom and spreads partners and opponents around — unless two players lock in as a Fixed Duo, in which case the pair moves through the queue as one welded unit.

Waiting players stay visible

Matches in progress and players still waiting are shown live — the queue view answers "when am I up?" without anyone asking the desk. Lock a player to hold their spot without being drafted, or check them out when they leave.

After the session

Ratings move, the leaderboard updates, and every completed match is ready to export to DUPR. Restart the session anytime — stats reset cleanly and sync safely across every organizer's device.
