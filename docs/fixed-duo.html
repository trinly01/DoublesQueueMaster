How Fixed Duo works

  • Link two players — the pair becomes one unit in the queue
  • The duo claims the later partner's queue position (no line-jumping)
  • Matchmaking drafts the pair as a team and fills the other side from solos or another duo
  • Checking out either partner breaks the link cleanly

And when two players should not team up?

The flip side exists too: Avoid Player lets admins mark players who shouldn't be on the same team — sibling rivalries, coaching situations, repeated friction. Matchmaking respects it as a hard rule.

Fair to everyone else

A welded pair doesn't get special treatment on timing — they wait their turn like everyone else; the lock only controls who they're teamed with. Combined with queue-priority modes and balanced matchmaking, the rest of the line stays fair.
