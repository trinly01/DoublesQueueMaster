DinkMatch — Pickleball Open Play Queue & Matchmaking App

DinkMatch is a free web app that runs fair pickleball open play sessions — it replaces the paddle rack and the whiteboard. Players check in, join a transparent first-in-line queue, and the app builds balanced matches automatically.

Fair queue and smart matchmaking

  • Transparent FIFO queue with configurable winner/loser re-entry and queue-priority modes (first in line, least played first)
  • Skill-balanced matchmaking modes: Pro Pick, Casual, Competitive, Standard, Social — plus Manual Match with a live team-balance indicator
  • Fixed duos stay welded together; avoid-player lists keep chosen players apart
  • Club ratings, leaderboards, Best Duo analysis, and player stats (partners, rivals, clutch record)
  • DUPR integration: connect your DUPR account, submit match results directly, or export a DUPR-ready CSV
  • Multi-device sync so admins and moderators run the same session together

How does it work?

Check players in, let the app draft the next four from the queue, send them to a court, and tap the winner. The queue re-orders itself, ratings update, and the leaderboard stays live — no sign-up needed to try open play mode.
