Three leaderboards

  • Club — your session's players, ranked by club rating
  • Global — the top-rated players across all DinkMatch clubs
  • Best Duo — pairs ranked by how they perform together

Every player gets a scouting profile

  • Rating — history chart over time
  • Matches — full game log
  • Partners — who you win most with, record together
  • Rivals — head-to-head records
  • Clutch — games decided by 2 points or less

Ratings that reflect skill, not attendance

The rating moves on opponent strength and score margin — beating a strong pair 11-3 means more than squeaking past beginners. In doubles, the stronger partner takes a modestly larger share of each result, so the board stays honest.
