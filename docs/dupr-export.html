The workflow

  1. Run open play — queue, matches, and scores live in DinkMatch.
  2. Players add their DUPR ID — stored in their profile, included automatically.
  3. Export the CSV — one file, all completed matches, DUPR-formatted.
  4. Upload in the DUPR club dashboard — results submitted in bulk.

Why not enter scores by hand?

A 30-person open play night is dozens of match results. Typing them into DUPR one by one takes an hour and invites errors. DinkMatch captures the score as part of ending the match — export is a free byproduct, not extra work.

DinkMatch ratings vs. DUPR

DinkMatch keeps its own club rating (starts at 1450, moves on opponent strength and score margin) for matchmaking and leaderboards — it's not a DUPR replacement. The CSV export is how your session results reach the official rating system.
