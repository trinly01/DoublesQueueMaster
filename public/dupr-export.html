The workflow

  1. Players connect DUPR — link your DUPR account in your profile to see your official singles/doubles ratings in-app.
  2. Run open play — queue, matches, and scores live in DinkMatch.
  3. Submit to DUPR — send completed matches directly, or export one DUPR-formatted CSV for bulk upload.

Organizers can also run DUPR+ sessions, where check-in requires an active DUPR subscription — useful for sanctioned or premium events.

Why not enter scores by hand?

A 30-person open play night is dozens of match results. Typing them into DUPR one by one takes an hour and invites errors. DinkMatch captures the score as part of ending the match — reporting to DUPR is a free byproduct, not extra work.

DinkMatch ratings vs. DUPR

DinkMatch keeps its own club rating (starts at 1450, moves on opponent strength and score margin) for matchmaking and leaderboards — it's not a DUPR replacement. Direct submission and the CSV export are how your session results reach the official rating system.
