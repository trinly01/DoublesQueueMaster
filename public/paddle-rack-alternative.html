What players actually see

A live board — on the desk tablet, a TV, or their own phones — showing who's playing, who's waiting, and who's next. Nobody hovers over the organizer asking "am I up?" because the answer is on the wall.

Keeps the good parts

Latecomers join mid-session. Players can sit out a round without losing their place forever. A pair that always plays together locks in as a Fixed Duo. The organizer can still override everything — Manual Match puts any four players on any court in two taps.
