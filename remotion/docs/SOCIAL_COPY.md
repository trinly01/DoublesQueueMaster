## Posting playbook

- **Pinned comment** (all posts):
  ```text
  Set up your club in 5 min — no credit card, no sign-up wall for players.
  ```
- **Best times**: 6–8am weekdays (before club play), 8–10pm (post-game scroll)
- **Boost order**: Ad 2 (ZeroPolitics) first — the organizer-pain hook
  targets the person who makes the signup decision
- **Format map**: `-9x16` → TikTok/Reels/Shorts · `-1x1` → FB/IG feed ·
  `-16x9` → YouTube pre-roll & landing page

## Variation notes

- Rotate hook emojis per repost to avoid ad-fatigue flags
- Keep `#pickleball` + `#openplay` on every post; swap 2–3 niche tags
  (`#dupr`, `#paddle`, `#pickleballaddict`) per variant
- UGC-style comment replies ("our club runs this now, game changer")
  outperform branded replies — seed with real club accounts if available
