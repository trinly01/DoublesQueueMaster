# Tournament Team Selection Report

**For:** coaching staff & team managers · **Prepared:** Nov 6, 2026

**The squad:** 10 players (5 men, 5 women) · **The format:** 1 Men's Doubles + 1 Women's Doubles + 3 Mixed Doubles pairs · **To advance:** the team must win at least **3 of the 5 pair matches**.

**What we analyzed:** every completed match our players have played — **1,747 matches** from June to November 2026, across all clubs. We counted **all** their accounts (some players have second accounts or played as guests before registering), so nothing was missed. All ratings were rebuilt from actual match results — we did not copy the numbers shown in the app.

**How much was checked:** there are exactly **600 possible ways** to arrange 10 players into these 5 pairs. **We tested all 600.** The complete ranked list is in `TOURNAMENT_ALL_COMBINATIONS.csv` — this report shows only our recommended picks.

> **How to read the numbers:** "Win chance" = how likely that pair is to win its match (0–100%). "Recent form" = win rate weighted toward recent matches — a win last week counts more than a win in June. "~" = estimate based on ratings, not proven results.
